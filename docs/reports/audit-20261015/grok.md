The journal does not store the raw reply, `elapsed_ms`, or token counts. `logs/state/event_news_rules_check.jsonl` has 16 rows, all `mismatch=true`, all T4, side `no`, from `2026-10-15T12:22:36Z` to `2026-10-15T13:42:32Z`. The YES-floor lines are `yes_below_floor ticker=KXTRUMPSAY-26OCT19-NOBE side=yes ask=43` (four times) and `ask=44` once.
